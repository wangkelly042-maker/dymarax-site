DymaraX / Research & Development

A Framework Should Earn the Right to Remain.

DymaraX is developing an analytical framework to examine the relationship between customer movement and the organizational systems supporting it.

Our research begins with controlled synthetic experiments designed to challenge the framework, expose analytical weaknesses, and document what the evidence does and does not support.

Three Journey Labs. Three successive framework versions tested. An evolving research record.
The research record

Three experiments. An evolving methodology.

Each Journey Lab tested a prospectively specified framework version using synthetic organizational evidence, independent analyses, and controlled comparison. Findings were preserved rather than retroactively rewritten to fit later revisions.

01 / FOUNDATIONAL EXPERIMENT

Journey Lab 001

Northstar Learning Solutions
Framework v1.0

The initial experiment examined whether independent analysts could apply the framework consistently while distinguishing meaningful organizational conditions from ordinary operating activity.

The findings supported continued development but exposed weaknesses in materiality thresholds and the treatment of adaptive work. Conventional analysis identified most of the same phenomena.

Current framework status
v1.3
PROSPECTIVELY FROZEN

Development continues.

DymaraX Analytical Framework v1.3 incorporates three bounded clarifications arising from Journey Lab 003. It passed independent implementation-conformance review and was subsequently frozen for prospective use.

Journey Lab 003 tested v1.2, not v1.3. The v1.3 freeze establishes version control and implementation conformance. It does not establish scientific validation, real-world reliability, comparative superiority, business impact, or ROI.

Research principles

Evidence before conclusions.

Challenge the framework.

Experiments are designed to expose false positives, false negatives, ambiguity, disagreement, and weaknesses in analytical decision boundaries.

Preserve the historical record.

Later revisions do not rewrite earlier experimental results. Each Journey Lab remains associated with the methodology version originally tested.

Separate agreement from correctness.

Independent analysts can agree and still reach an unsupported conclusion. Agreement is therefore examined alongside evidence quality and classification accuracy.

Respect the limits of the evidence.

Synthetic methodology-development experiments cannot independently establish real-world effectiveness, external validity, predictive performance, or organizational return on investment.
