Privacy Policy

DymaraX respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, communicate with us, or express interest in our work.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. About DymaraX

DymaraX develops analytical approaches for examining customer journeys and the organizational systems that support them.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit dymarax.com or contact DymaraX.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, information may include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us, such as your name, email address, and message.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device category, operating system, and approximate geographic location.
  • Website usage information, including pages visited, referring websites, and interactions with website content.
  • Information collected through analytics technologies, subject to applicable privacy and consent requirements.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, understand website performance, improve accessibility and usability, maintain website security, and evaluate interest in DymaraX's research and services.

4. Website Analytics

DymaraX uses Vercel Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and performance.

DymaraX also plans to use Microsoft Clarity to better understand website interactions through features such as heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity will not be activated until the relevant privacy and consent configuration has been reviewed.

Analytics providers may process technical and usage information according to their respective privacy policies and service terms.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Some website technologies may use cookies or similar mechanisms to support analytics and functionality. Where consent is legally required, optional analytics technologies will be subject to the applicable consent choices.

You may also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

6. Sharing of Information

DymaraX may share information with service providers that support website hosting, analytics, security, and communications, where necessary for those purposes.

DymaraX does not currently operate an independent business model based on selling visitor personal information.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal obligations and the retention settings of relevant service providers.

8. International Visitors

Visitors may access this website from different countries. Information may be processed in countries other than the visitor's country of residence, depending on the service providers involved.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a privacy-related request, contact contact@dymarax.com . We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

10. Security

DymaraX takes reasonable steps to protect information handled through its website and service providers. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as DymaraX's website, services, and information practices develop. Updates will be reflected by the date displayed at the top of this page.
